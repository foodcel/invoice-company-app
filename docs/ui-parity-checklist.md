# Tauri UI parity and acceptance checklist

**Reference set:** `mockups/index.html` (the active **Accueil calme** / **Professionnel classique** combination), `docs/app-brief.md`, and `docs/numbering-and-save-options.md`, all as supplied on 2026-09-28. This is a **checklist for the new Windows app**, not a claim that any criterion has passed. Compare the running app and a produced customer document against the active mockup. Earlier design alternatives are outside this checklist.

Mark each box only after exercising the stated behavior. Capture screenshots of the two document types in light and dark mode at wide and narrow window sizes, plus an actual printed page and PDF. For behavior, record the input, result, and persisted result after restart. **P0** blocks a usable release or risks incorrect customer documents; **P1** is visual/interaction parity or recovery quality. Unresolved business rules at the end require Dad's answer before final live-accounting sign-off.

## 1. Opening and view states

- [ ] **P1 — Calm opening view.** Show the Ébénisterie de l'Hermitage inc. brand with its symbol and company name in real text, a prominent French question, and two large, obvious **Soumission** and **Facture** choices. Keep **Documents récents** and the light/dark control visible. A fresh install must not prefill any sample customer. The active mockup places the choices above an initially blank soumission editor; if the app uses a separate choice screen as described in the brief, compare the choices and resulting editor states rather than requiring that exact navigation layout.
- [ ] **P0 — Type-specific editor state.** Selecting Soumission shows **Valide jusqu’au**, quote labels, and no public quote number. Selecting Facture shows the optional **Date limite de paiement**, invoice labels, and a conspicuous invoice number in editor and preview before export. The date fields occupy the same row without an extra helper line under the invoice date.
- [ ] **P1 — Type switch.** Switch Soumission → Facture → Soumission with entered project, client, addresses, notes, deposit, and multiple lines. The current draft's text stays, the document-specific date and deposit labels change, the customer preview changes, and a short transition does not steal focus or erase data. Switching the type of the current draft must not silently create a second saved quote or an issued invoice.
- [ ] **P1 — Empty, editing, error, review, saved, and recent states.** Verify the blank form/preview, populated form/preview, validation errors, line dictation/rewrite proposal, recent-documents dialog, empty and no-match search states, save feedback, and storage failure feedback. Do not show a success state for an output that was not created.

## 2. Form structure and direct editing

- [ ] **P1 — Reading order and geometry.** Editor order is full-width **Nom du projet**; paired document date and valid-until/payment-due date; full-width required **Nom du client**; paired optional phone and email; full-width billing address; full-width optional delivery address; work lines; full-row optional deposit; full-row optional client note; language action; adjacent **Créer le PDF** / **Imprimer** actions. There is no unexplained hard-coded **Objet** section, repeated tax-inclusive total in the editor, or PDF-name/location card.
- [ ] **P1 — Date controls.** New document date defaults to today and can be changed to a past or future date. A new soumission's valid-until date starts 30 days later, remains editable, and is required for output. A new facture's due date starts blank; preview reads **Aucune** until selected and again after clearing. Calendar buttons open a rounded in-app calendar with month/year selection, previous/next month, today, and **Aucune date** for invoice due date. Values, preview, and reopened draft agree. Reject impossible dates.
- [ ] **P1 — Field sizing.** Address inputs remain one line high and do not resize. Description starts at two lines and grows with text; client note starts at one line and grows. Description paragraphs are justified in editor and preview without stretching the final line. Notes, descriptions, quantity, and price use the same readable input text size as the other fields. Long text remains visible after editing, switching types, reopening, printing, and exporting.
- [ ] **P1 — Work lines.** A blank draft has one line, quantity `1`, editable description and typed quantity/price fields. **Ajouter une ligne** follows the last line; new rows have their own number, two discreet icon actions in the line header, and an always-visible rounded-square remove **X** at the header's top right. Adding/removing renumbers lines, updates preview and calculations, and never leaves the editor without a blank line. A row's amount is adjacent to its quantity and price.
- [ ] **P0 — Calculation and validation.** As values change, show each line amount, subtotal, TPS **5 %**, TVQ **9,975 %**, and **Total avec taxes** in CAD with consistent cent rounding. Output requires a valid document date, client name, billing address, and at least one complete work line (description, quantity > 0, price >= 0); soumission also requires valid-until. Invoice due date, project, phone, email, delivery address, deposit, and note stay optional. A zero price is valid; blank, nonnumeric, negative, or zero quantity is not. Both output actions use the same checks, list missing fields in French, mark them, and move focus to the first problem.
- [ ] **P0 — Deposit display.** Blank or zero deposit produces no deposit/balance rows on the customer document. Positive deposit shows **Dépôt demandé / Solde après dépôt** for a soumission or **Dépôt reçu / Solde à payer** for a facture. Changing type updates both labels and totals. Resolve the actual tax applicability and deposit rules before live financial use; the mockup's arithmetic is a prototype example.

## 3. Customer preview and visual parity

- [ ] **P1 — Selected composition.** At wide sizes, keep the roomy two-choice area and a large Letter-proportioned customer preview beside the editor, with the preview given substantial width. The white page uses the **Professionnel classique** composition: company symbol and real-text name on the left, document type/number on the right, project/date strip, client and issuer cards, item table, optional note at lower left, totals at lower right, and tax IDs in the footer.
- [ ] **P0 — Customer facts.** Issuer reads **Ébénisterie de l'Hermitage inc.**, **68, chemin des guides, Ripon, Qc J0V 1V0**, **(819) 428-7690**. Footer shows **TPS 848045563 RT0001** and **TVQ 1212260726 TQ0001**. Use only the logo's symbol as an image; the company name remains selectable text. Preview and PDF use the actual entered client, addresses, project, dates, lines, notes, and assigned invoice number. Optional phone, email, delivery address, and notes appear only when filled. Empty-form placeholder text must not enter a customer PDF.
- [ ] **P1 — Page growth.** The screen preview starts with US Letter **8.5 × 11** proportion and expands vertically for long descriptions or many items. Notes remain at lower left, totals in the lower-right area when feasible, and the footer follows the content without covering rows. Test a genuinely long document; the final PDF needs real multipage Letter pagination with no clipping, overlay, or orphaned totals/footer. The mockup does not specify a maximum line count.
- [ ] **P1 — Colors and feedback.** Compare light and dark editor states to the warm off-white/green active mockup, including active choice, controls, borders, focus, disabled state, validation red, subtle hover/press, and short type-switch animation. Keep the customer page white and legible in either theme. Colors are provisional per the brief, so record approved changes instead of treating every hexadecimal value as immutable.
- [ ] **P1 — Window widths.** Check roughly 1440, 1250, 760, and 600 CSS-pixel-equivalent widths, plus the smallest supported Windows window. Around 1250, editor and preview stack; around 760, the client fields become one column and the preview stays available with horizontal scrolling if necessary; around 600, top controls wrap. The selected mockup collapses Save to an icon when the editor container is narrow. No clipped labels, overlapping header icons, inaccessible actions, or hidden content. Check Windows display scaling at 100 %, 150 %, and 200 %.

## 4. Print, PDF, names, and numbering

- [ ] **P0 — Distinct output actions.** **Imprimer** opens a Windows print flow containing only the customer document, on US Letter with suitable margins; no editor, app header, choice buttons, toast, preview caption, modal, or preview-only **aperçu** tag appears. **Créer le PDF** creates an actual, non-editable customer-copy PDF and reports its saved location or an actionable error. The mockup only validates the PDF button and opens browser print for Print; its PDF toast is not implementation acceptance.
- [ ] **P0 — One general PDF intake folder.** Create/use the agreed general folder (proposed `Documents/Entreprise/À classer/`) without making Dad pick a client folder on every export. Generate the filename from the entered client name, selected document date, and assigned invoice number for factures; no public number for soumissions. Sanitize unsupported filename characters, avoid collisions between same-name/date quotes, and preserve a path/history record if a later sorting agent moves the file. An uncertain client match must remain in the intake folder for review.
- [ ] **P0 — Draft versus issued invoice.** An internal draft ID survives repeated Save clicks and restart. The user confirmed **2060 is next** on 2026-09-28. Show the next proposed number and allow a confirmed change for future unissued invoices without renumbering issued ones. The proposed number is visible before export; successful first issuance fixes it to the invoice record. Saving, reprinting, re-exporting, and retrying an interrupted/failed export reuse that invoice's number. Only a genuinely new invoice after issuance advances once. The stored record is authoritative, not the filename. Do not silently replace an issued PDF with a correction.
- [ ] **P0 — Print/PDF parity.** With the same valid draft, compare printed output and PDF page by page: text, line wrapping, amounts, tax IDs, number, dates, notes, page breaks, and company mark match the preview's customer content. A final printed invoice must have a fixed, recorded number; resolve whether first Print issues it or requires PDF issuance first. Reprints must not advance numbering, and failed PDF creation must not consume a second number.

## 5. Saving, recent documents, undo, and backup

- [ ] **P0 — Local draft persistence.** Typing, leaving a field, adding/removing lines, changing dates/type, accepting a proposal, and clicking **Enregistrer le brouillon** persist the same editable draft. The mockup uses about 500 ms idle autosave and immediate structural/focus-loss saves; the Tauri app should give equivalent protection. Close/reopen the app and recover the current content, line order, selected dates, and document identity. Clearly surface write failures instead of claiming a save succeeded.
- [ ] **P1 — Recent list and versions.** **Documents récents** opens a searchable list by client or project, with type, name, updated time, current-document indication, and clear empty/no-match states. Reopen a saved document without overwriting another one. Provide **Restaurer une version précédente** when one exists; restoring a version leaves an undoable path back to the state just before restore. The browser mockup keeps only a few versions in local storage (up to four per document), which is not a separate backup.
- [ ] **P1 — Undo/reset.** Undo starts disabled and restores the previous field edit, line add/remove, accepted description rewrite, reset, or type switch during the current session. **Réinitialiser** asks for confirmation, starts a blank draft of the same type, and leaves prior saved documents in Recents. Cancel leaves everything intact. Reset must not delete issued PDFs or reuse an assigned invoice number.
- [ ] **P0 — Independent recovery copy.** The Windows app keeps a separate recoverable backup of drafts/records, not just a second version in the same store. Test restoring after corruption or deletion of the primary local store while the backup remains. Before release, specify backup location, retention, frequency, restore UX, and how to avoid overwriting the last good copy with corrupted data. A browser-local mockup save does not satisfy this criterion.

## 6. Voice, line assistance, and English copy: capability boundary

- [ ] **P0 — Honest whole-document voice state.** The current mockup's **Remplir en parlant** only shows a future-feature notice. For the shipping app, either implement the brief's real flow (speech fills a reviewable draft, a spoken reply is given, and every field can be corrected by hand before output) or present the control as clearly unavailable. Never imply that voice filled or verified a document when it did not. Manual entry and both output actions remain usable without microphone access.
- [ ] **P1 — Per-line review UI.** Each line's microphone is labeled **Dicter cette ligne** and sparkle **Améliorer avec l’IA** through accessible names and hover labels. The existing description textarea is the only proposal field; it glows while listening and shows the temporary text for review. Below it, show **Texte clair** (default), **Liste à puces**, **Autre version**, and **Accepter** in the appropriate states. Pressing the active icon again cancels and restores the unaccepted original; pressing the mic during listening ends capture and then shows the proposal. **Autre version** keeps the chosen presentation. Until **Accepter**, neither the customer preview nor saved description changes. Acceptance affects only that line's description, never quantity, price, another line, or the whole document.
- [ ] **P0 — No invented work details.** The mockup's line formatter is local text formatting and browser speech recognition when available; it has no connected AI model. If the Tauri app advertises real AI, compare its proposed text to Dad's supplied words and reject added materials, quantities, work steps, or promises. Expose microphone/AI errors and fallback to typing. Test cancellation, empty speech, denied permission, and unavailable service without data loss or false acceptance.
- [ ] **P0 — English copy state.** The current **Traduire en anglais** action only explains a future flow. A real shipping translation must cover headings, descriptions, and notes, then show a separate editable English customer copy for review before its PDF. The French draft remains intact; amounts, tax rates, invoice number, dates, and registration IDs remain identical. Distinguish the English filename, for example with `EN`. Until implemented, label the action as future/unavailable rather than claiming translation occurred.

## 7. Accessibility and usability

- [ ] **P1 — Keyboard and screen reader.** All choices, fields, line actions, calendar, recent list, theme, Save, Undo, Reset, PDF, and Print work in a logical keyboard order. Icon-only actions have French accessible names and visible hover labels; focused controls have a strong visible ring in both themes. Announce save/error status and validation problems; errors identify the field and focus moves to the first invalid control.
- [ ] **P1 — Dialog and calendar focus.** In the recent-documents dialog, focus enters the search field, remains within the modal while open, closes by Escape or its close control, and returns to the opener. Calendar opens from field or button, supports keyboard month/year/day selection, closes by Escape, restores focus, and exposes the chosen date and expanded state to assistive technology. Check 200 % display scaling and large text without loss of controls.
- [ ] **P1 — Reduced motion and contrast.** Respect the operating system's reduced-motion setting: disable the type-switch animation, listening pulse, and button motion. Check text/control contrast and disabled/selected states in light and dark themes, and do not rely on color alone for errors or active choices. Keep the interface in simple French with large click/touch targets.
